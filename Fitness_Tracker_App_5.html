Your details

Why 1g of protein per lb of bodyweight? Research on muscle protein synthesis generally shows benefits up to around 0.7-1g per lb (roughly 1.6-2.2g/kg), with little extra benefit above that. It's set toward the higher end here because during a calorie deficit, more protein helps protect muscle mass while you lose fat — and it's a round, easy number to track consistently rather than a precise biological cutoff.
